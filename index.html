About & Privacy

MicroTools Hub exists because everyday tools shouldn't demand an account, show a 30-second ad, or ship your text to a server.

Every calculator and converter on this page runs locally in JavaScript inside your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere: no analytics, no cookies, no server requests after the page loads. Turn off your internet after opening this page and everything still works.
